================ VERDICT ================
Historical BARS work; historical QUOTES do not (no bid/ask history).
That is a real constraint, not a blocker: spreads would have to be priced from
trade closes rather than the mid, which is less accurate for illiquid strikes
and cannot reproduce the liquidity flag. Section 2 above shows how far back
bars reach. Worth doing as a rough first pass; marketdata.app stays the
accurate option.
