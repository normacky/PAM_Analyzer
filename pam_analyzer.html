What the labels mean
UC1 / UC2 — Up Continuation (long, Lessons 16/17). Up flow above the 50sma. v0.21 walks the deck structure back from the EXE bar: first peak → LP line → second peak. Gates: no flush-up bar in the 3 bars into the first peak, structure near/testing the 20/50 SMA, bullish EXE reclaims the LP line within 5 bars and still closes below the second peak. The tier is the deck's only real discriminator — UC2 when the second peak finishes above the first, UC1 when it falls short. Ties inside 0.10 ATR default to UC1. Both tiers trade identically; only the label differs.
DC1 / DC2 — Down Continuation (short). Exact mirror of UC: down flow, structure walked back as first trough → LP line → second trough, no flush-down bar into the first trough, near/testing the 20/50 SMA, bearish EXE rejects the LP within 5 bars and still closes above the second trough. DC2 when the second trough finishes below the first, DC1 when it falls short.
UR1 / UR2 — Reversal shorts (Lessons 9/19). Both require a majority flush-up MOVE into the LP swing-high — steep price-movement angle (a series of bars or a gap qualifies; ≥1.5×ATR per bar here) starting at/beyond the 50% line of X (the LP) to Y (the lowest point right of X). Without that flush there is no reversal label at all. UR1 is the general case and is valid anywhere on the chart — no SMA reference (per the lesson video). UR2 is the subset at the end of an uptrend: up flow above the 50 SMA plus a retracement bigger than the last completed prior one; if the prior can't be measured, the bar stays UR1. Bearish EXE closes at/below the LP within 5 bars.
DR1 / DR2 — Reversal longs. Exact mirror: both require a majority flush-down move into the LP swing-low. DR1 = the general case, valid anywhere in any flow. DR2 = the subset at the end of a downtrend: down flow below the 50 SMA plus a verified bigger retracement than the prior one (unverifiable → stays DR1). Bullish EXE recovers the LP within 5 bars. A slow grind into the LP is a wave, not a flush, and produces no label.
Bull-FS / Bear-FS — Force Strike (course Lesson 8). Mother/baby inside-bar pair is mandatory and consecutive: a mother bar, then the single baby bar immediately after it inside the mother's range. Then a single Force Strike bar — a same-direction EXE (PIN / Ice Cream / Mark Up-Down) — pierces the mother's extreme intrabar and closes back inside on that same bar, occurring within 3 bars of the baby. Any flow, above or below the 50sma. If the pierce also dipped through a live LP and the EXE closed back at/on the correct side of it, that's the force bottom/top scenario — the standalone-tradeable case; without an LP it's a formation flag only. There is no FS without the inside pair.
Force top / bottom — price pierces an un-breached swing high/low (LP) then closes back on the correct side of it: a failed break = a trap. LPs come from the full loaded history; the pierce may be any of the last 5 bars. A swing level needs 3 higher/lower bars to its LEFT and 1 to its right (v0.19: left precedence, plus one bar of confirmation) and dies once any bar closes through it.
EXE bar — the candle traded off: PIN, Ice Cream or Mark up/down. Flush bars are exits, not entries.
1 vs 2 — the "2" is always the narrower case: for continuations a deeper two-leg pullback, for reversals a majority flush + bigger retracement. Every "2" is also a "1". Geometry is approximated, not slide-exact — eyeball the structure to confirm.
